The comfort you'll feel (not counted in payback)

Payback only counts dollars saved on your energy bill. A properly insulated attic also delivers:

  • Cooler summers. New Mexico attics can top 130°F. A full R-49 blanket keeps that heat out of your ceilings and living space.
  • More even temperatures. Fewer hot bedrooms, cold rooms and fights over the thermostat.
  • Warmer, less drafty winters. Air sealing closes gaps where warm air escapes into the attic.
  • Less wear on your HVAC. Your furnace and cooling system don't have to run as much, which can help them last longer.
  • A quieter home. Thick insulation softens outside noise like traffic, wind and planes.

Savings estimate: about 45% of a typical New Mexico energy bill goes to heating and cooling. ENERGY STAR estimates sealing and insulating saves an average of 15% of heating and cooling costs; we scale that by how much insulation you have today. Assumes today's energy prices. Your actual savings will vary.

Rebates are paid by your utility after installation and are subject to program rules and funding. NM Gas Company: must heat with natural gas, work installed by a participating contractor between April 1, 2026 and March 31, 2027, application within 30 days. PNM: requires a PNM Home Energy Checkup before purchase. The NM tax credit is claimed on your state return and requires EMNRD certification. We'll help you confirm eligibility at your inspection.
