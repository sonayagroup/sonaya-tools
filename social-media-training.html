📚 Content Marketing Fundamentals Day 1 — PM

Core Concepts

  • The algorithm rewards watch time, saves, and shares — not likes
  • Hook viewers in the first 1–2 seconds or they scroll past
  • Consistency beats virality — 5 solid posts/week outperforms 1 viral hit then silence
  • Authentic > polished — phone-shot vertical video outperforms studio content for local brands
  • Every post needs a job: make them hungry (DT), make them worried about their attic (Koala), or make them trust us
  • Captions matter — most people watch without sound, so text overlays are mandatory
  • Hashtags are discovery tools, not decoration — use the brand-specific sets in the Command Center

💬 Brand Voice Guidelines Day 3 — AM

Each brand has a distinct voice. Never mix them.

DEL TACO Voice: Crave-worthy, proud, local

Speak like a friend who just found the best late-night spot, not a corporate marketing team. Be proud of the product — freshly hand-grated cheese, fresh tomatoes, flame-grilled. Short punchy captions. No paragraphs. Make it snackable.

Do

  • Lead with the food — the visual IS the hook
  • Use local slang and NM references naturally
  • Mention Del Yeah! Rewards when natural (not forced)
  • Feature real team members and customers
  • Use brand hashtags: #deltaco #delyeahrewards #newmexicofood

Don't

  • Sound corporate or scripted
  • Make green chile the only story — it's ONE part of a full menu
  • Post blurry, dark, or unappetizing food photos — ever
  • Use generic stock photography
  • Post without text overlay/captions on video

KOALA Voice: Expert but fun, local, approachable

You're the neighbor who happens to know everything about attics — not a salesperson. Use the show format: every attic is Red (bad), Green (good), or Christmas (mixed). Always close with: "We like our green chile hot. Not our attics."

Do

  • Lead with thermal camera reveals — the temperature difference IS the hook
  • Feature the real crew by name — Gabriel, Mike, Frank, Jacob, Mark, Ricardo, Joel
  • Connect insulation to money saved, comfort gained, energy bills reduced
  • Include Spanish-language content (Mark, Ricardo, Michelle, crew)

Don't

  • Be boring or overly technical — "your attic is 140°F" beats "R-value"
  • Skip the verdict — every inspection needs a Red/Green/Christmas call
  • Use the mascot in every video — ~1 in 3 pieces, deadpan background only
  • Post without the sign-off line on show episodes
  • Post Koala content with Del Taco branding or vice versa
📈 Analytics & Reporting Day 3 — PM

  • TikTok: Profile → Creator tools → Analytics → Content

  • YouTube: Studio → Analytics → Content → Shorts tab (subscriber count syncs automatically in Command Center)
TIP: Screenshot your top 3 posts each week and save them in a "Weekly Wins" folder on Google Drive. This builds a library of what works. Also log those metrics in the Command Center so the Analytics dashboard stays current.
📅 Daily & Weekly Workflow Day 3 — PM

Daily Routine

  1. Check the Social Command Center Briefing — see what's due today
  2. Review the post's notes for production guidance and shot details
  3. Film / edit / prep the day's content
  4. Add captions/text overlay in CapCut
  5. Write the caption using the brand voice guidelines
  6. Upload media to Google Drive, copy the shareable link as the Media URL
  7. Click "⚡ Publish Now" in the Command Center to post to Instagram & Facebook. Post natively to TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
  8. Respond to comments and DMs within 2 hours during business hours
  9. Save any UGC / customer posts that tag us

Weekly Routine

  1. Monday: Review the week's content calendar. Identify any gaps.
  2. Tuesday–Thursday: Primary filming and editing days
  3. Friday: Collect raw footage from Del Taco store managers (Google Drive)
  4. Friday afternoon: Prep and batch-edit content for the following Mon–Wed
  5. End of week: Pull analytics and screenshot top 3 posts per brand
  6. Weekly check-in with Pranav: what performed, what's coming, any blockers

Monthly Routine

  1. Review monthly themes (see Command Center Themes section)
  2. Plan special content around upcoming themes or events
  3. Content audit: which pillars are underrepresented? Rebalance.
  4. Update the pipeline: move ideas → draft → review → approved
🎦 Filming Day Checklist Reference

Equipment Bag

  • DJI Osmo Pocket 3 — charged, SD card inserted and formatted
  • DJI Mic 2 — both transmitters and receiver charged
  • Wind screens for DJI Mic 2 (if shooting outdoors)
  • Phone — connected to DJI Mimo app for monitoring
  • Portable charger / extra batteries
  • Microfiber cloth for lens
  • Small tripod / mount (optional, for static shots)

Before You Shoot

  • Review the shot list in the Command Center for today's content
  • Read the production notes in the post's "notes" field
  • Check lighting conditions — natural light for food (DT), thermal camera for attics (Koala)
  • Test audio levels with DJI Mic 2 before the real take
  • Shoot in portrait (9:16) for social — double check before recording
  • Get multiple takes — you can cut in CapCut, but you can't reshoot from the office

After You Shoot

  • Review footage on the Pocket 3 screen — check focus, audio, framing
  • Transfer clips to your editing machine
  • Back up raw footage to Google Drive
  • Log what you shot and any notes for editing

How Direct Publishing Works

  1. Create or edit a post in the Command Center — fill in caption, hashtags, media URL, and select platforms
  2. Click the green "⚡ Publish Now" button in the modal footer
  3. The Command Center sends the caption + hashtags + media to each connected platform's API
  4. The post status automatically updates to "Posted" with a timestamp
  5. Success/failure toasts appear for each platform — if one fails, others still publish
NOTE: Instagram image posts require a publicly accessible image URL. Upload to Google Drive, set sharing to "Anyone with the link," and use that URL as the Media URL. Reels and video content still need to be posted natively through the Instagram app.
📱 Platform-by-Platform Posting Guide Reference

You can now publish directly from the Social Command Center to connected platforms using the "⚡ Publish Now" button. For platforms not yet connected, continue posting natively.

Publishing from the Command Center (Instagram & Facebook)

  • Create your post in the Command Center with caption, hashtags, media URL, and platform selections
  • Click "⚡ Publish Now" to push directly to Instagram and/or Facebook via API
  • Instagram requires an image URL — host the image on Google Drive or another public URL first

  • The post status automatically changes to "Posted" with a timestamp
  • After publishing, still add to IG Story with a "New post!" sticker or poll (manual step)

  • Save any tagged UGC to a collection for reposting
TIP: Upload your media to Google Drive first, get a shareable link, and paste it as the Media URL. The Command Center sends it directly to Instagram/Facebook when you hit Publish Now.

Instagram (Additional Tips)

  • Post Reels for all video content (not regular feed videos) — Reels require native posting for now
