Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sonaya Group LLC and its affiliated entities ("Sonaya Group," "we," "us," or "our") collect, use, and share information when you use our applications, websites, and tools hosted at tools.sonayagroup.com (the "Services"), including the Social Command Center and related integrations.

Information We Collect

When you use our Services, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and social media account credentials needed to connect your accounts. We also collect information automatically through your use of the Services, including browser type, device information, and usage data such as pages visited and actions taken.

When you connect third-party accounts (such as Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, or Google Drive), we receive access tokens that allow the Services to interact with those platforms on your behalf. We store these tokens locally in your browser and do not transmit them to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide and operate the Services, including publishing and scheduling social media content on your behalf, retrieving analytics from connected platforms, and managing media files through connected storage services. We also use information to improve the Services and respond to your requests.

Third-Party Services

Our Services integrate with third-party platforms including Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google (YouTube and Google Drive), and TikTok. When you connect these accounts, your use of those platforms remains subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service. We access only the data and permissions you explicitly authorize.

Data Storage and Security

API credentials and access tokens are stored locally in your web browser using browser storage mechanisms. Post content and scheduling data are stored in our secure database infrastructure. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services. You can disconnect any third-party account at any time through the Services' connection settings, which removes the stored access tokens from your browser. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the address below.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You may disconnect third-party accounts at any time. You may also request a copy of the data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated effective date.
