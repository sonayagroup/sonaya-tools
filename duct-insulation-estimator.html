Duct runs Example job, replace with yours

Round: 3.14 × (diameter ÷ 12) × length. Rectangular: (W × 2 + H × 2) ÷ 12 × length. Flat oval: (3.14 × H + 2 × (W − H)) ÷ 12 × length, with H the short side. All sizes in inches, lengths in feet. A "24 inch duct" means 24" diameter.

Material settings

Spacers: one strip wraps around the duct every 24", with a strip at each end. Tape: one seam along the full length of each run, plus a seam around the duct every 4 ft where rolls butt together (rolls are 48" wide). The overlap and cuts percentage is added to insulation, spacers and tape. Settings and the labor rate are saved in this browser.
