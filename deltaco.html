Fixed costs include payroll, occupancy, utilities, maintenance, insurance, interest, plus equal corporate overhead allocation ($4,278/store/mo). Payroll treated as fully fixed (simplification — actual break-even likely lower). Revenue is projected from available daily sales data when the month is incomplete.
