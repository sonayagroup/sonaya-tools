How to Update Prices (click to expand)

This page shows your common materials alongside prices from each supplier (IDI, RIS, Cameron Ashley, Lowe's, and Misc).

1. Hover over any supplier price and click "Use" to select that supplier for an item.
2. Or use Bulk Set Source above the table to set all items in a category at once.
3. Click "Save All Changes" to write your selections to the database.
4. The estimator automatically pulls the saved prices when building quotes.
Need to update a supplier's price? Double-click any price cell to edit it.
Made a mistake? Click "Undo" before saving to revert your last change.
