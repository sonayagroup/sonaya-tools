The Problem

Starting a business in New Mexico shouldn't require a law degree

📋

License Labyrinth

New Mexico has no single license portal. Requirements are scattered across the Secretary of State, RLD, Taxation & Revenue, and your local city — and they vary by industry, entity type, and location.

💰

Expensive Attorneys

Attorneys charge $1,500–$3,000+ just for LLC formation and basic operating agreements. That's money better spent on your actual business.

📊

Vendor Overwhelm

Payroll, POS, bookkeeping, insurance, banking — dozens of options for each, and the wrong choice costs you time, money, and integration headaches.

⚠️

Gross Receipts Tax Confusion

New Mexico's GRT isn't a standard sales tax — rates vary by city (7.06%–8.31%) and many new owners register incorrectly or miss filing deadlines.
